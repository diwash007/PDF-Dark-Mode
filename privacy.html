Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

PDF Dark Mode is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

Information Collection

PDF Dark Mode does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal information.

The extension operates locally within your browser and processes PDF rendering settings directly on your device.

Local Storage

The extension may locally store the following preferences on your device:

  • Dark mode settings
  • Contrast and filter preferences
  • Selected reading modes
  • Per-site allow/block rules
  • Temporary entitlement or Pro status flags

This information is stored locally using browser storage APIs and is not transmitted to external servers.

Analytics

PDF Dark Mode may provide local-only usage summaries inside the extension popup for user convenience.

No browsing history, PDF content, personal information, or usage data is sent to external analytics services.

Payments

If Pro features are offered, payments may be processed through third-party payment providers such as Lemon Squeezy, Paddle, Dodo Payments, Stripe, or similar services.

Payment information is handled directly by the payment provider and is subject to their respective privacy policies. PDF Dark Mode does not store credit card or banking information.

Permissions

The extension may request browser permissions required for:

  • Applying dark mode to PDF documents
  • Storing user preferences
  • Managing per-site configuration rules

Permissions are only used for extension functionality and not for advertising or tracking purposes.

Third-Party Services

PDF Dark Mode may link to external websites such as GitHub, browser extension stores, or payment providers. These services operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

PDF Dark Mode is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or operational improvements.
