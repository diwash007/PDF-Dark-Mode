PDF Dark Mode

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

PDF Dark Mode is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

Information Collection

PDF Dark Mode does not collect, store, transmit, or sell your browsing history, PDF content, keystrokes, or precise identifiers.

The extension operates locally within your browser and processes PDF rendering settings directly on your device. The only data that ever leaves your device is the Pro license information described below.

Pro Licenses

Activating a Pro license sends your license key plus a coarse browser tag (browser family and operating system only, e.g. chrome-windows — never versions, history, or page contents) to Lemon Squeezy for activation. The tag becomes the label of that license instance in our Lemon Squeezy dashboard so Pro usage can be understood by browser. No notice is shown in the extension itself; this policy is the disclosure.

License Validation

To keep Pro status accurate, the extension re-validates a stored license with Lemon Squeezy (at api.lemonsqueezy.com) roughly every 24 hours while the browser runs. Each check sends only the license key, its instance ID, and the instance label. If the network is unreachable, validation is skipped and Pro features stay enabled — a failed connection never revokes a paid license.

Web Reader

The web reader at pdf-dark.com/reader.html processes PDFs entirely in your browser. Uploaded files are never transmitted to a server and are discarded from memory when you close the page.

Local Storage

The extension may locally store the following preferences on your device:

  • Dark mode settings
  • Contrast and filter preferences
  • Selected reading modes
  • Per-site allow/block rules
  • Full-dark viewer preferences (background theme and options-panel state)
  • License and entitlement record (license key, instance ID, browser tag, validation timestamps)

This information is stored locally using browser storage APIs. Only the license record described above is ever transmitted, and only to Lemon Squeezy for activation and validation.

Analytics

PDF Dark Mode may provide local-only usage summaries inside the extension popup for user convenience.

No browsing history, PDF content, personal information, or usage data is sent to external analytics services.

Payments

Pro payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy as the merchant of record.

Payment information is handled directly by Lemon Squeezy and is subject to the Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy. PDF Dark Mode does not store credit card or banking information.

Permissions

The extension may request browser permissions required for:

  • Applying dark mode to PDF documents
  • Storing user preferences
  • Managing per-site configuration rules

Permissions are only used for extension functionality and not for advertising or tracking purposes.

Third-Party Services

PDF Dark Mode relies on Lemon Squeezy for payments and license management (see above), and may link to external websites such as GitHub or browser extension stores. These services operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

PDF Dark Mode is not directed toward children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or operational improvements.
