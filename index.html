Journal of Human–Computer Studies · Vol. 47

Visual Fatigue in Extended Screen Reading

R. Mehta, K. Lindqvist, A. Osei — Received 14 March

Abstract

Prolonged exposure to high-luminance documents is associated with measurable increases in reported ocular discomfort. We examine reading sessions exceeding ninety minutes across 214 participants and find that perceived strain correlates more strongly with background luminance than with total session duration.

1. Introduction

Document formats designed for print carry an implicit assumption: that the page reflects light rather than emitting it. On a backlit display this inverts the ergonomics entirely.

4.2 Method

FAQ

Questions worth answering.

Is $9.99 really the only payment?

Yes. There is no renewal, no expiry date, and no upgrade tier waiting behind it. Pro features that ship in future versions are included in the same licence.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for. Checkout gives you a licence key, you paste it into the extension, and that's the entire process.

Can I use my licence on more than one browser?

Activate the same key in the extension on each browser you read in. If an activation is refused because you've reached the limit on your key, email me and I'll sort it out.

Does it work on PDFs saved on my computer?

It does, but Chrome and Edge block extensions from local files by default. You'll need to switch on Allow access to file URLs — the help page walks through it with screenshots.

What does the extension do with my documents?

Nothing leaves your machine. The overlay is drawn locally over the page you're already viewing, and the usage counters stay on-device. Details are in the privacy policy.

If it's open source, why pay at all?

The whole extension is GPL-3.0 and the source is on GitHub — you're welcome to build it yourself. Paying for a licence is how the maintained, store-published version keeps getting worked on.

Can I get a refund?

Refund requests are reviewed case by case. If it isn't working the way you expected, get in touch before anything else — most problems turn out to be a settings fix. See the terms for the full policy.
